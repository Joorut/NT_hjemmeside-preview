Privacy & cookies

What data do we collect?

When you contact us, send an enquiry or apply for a job, we process the information you provide: name, contact details and a description of the job.

Why?

We use the data to answer your enquiry, deliver the agreed service, invoice and comply with the law.

Cookies

The website does not use tracking or marketing cookies, so we do not show a cookie banner. Fonts and images are served from our own server.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data. Contact us at nuuk@transport.gl.
