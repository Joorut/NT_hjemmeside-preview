We are Greenland’s largest waste collector and handle most of the household refuse in Nuuk with modern, environmentally sound equipment.

Refuse & containers

Containers for homes and businesses

When we took over refuse collection in Nuuk, the municipality’s total costs were halved, thanks to new compactor trucks and smaller containers emptied more often.

We rent out and empty:

  • Mini containers of 140 L, 350 L and 660 L
  • Midi containers for household and combustible waste
  • Open containers for construction waste (metal and wood)
  • Durable wooden bin stands (for sale)

Households with bin stands register for collection with Kommuneqarfik Sermersooq. Container emptying is booked directly with us on +299 32 24 80 (8am–4pm).

Services

Bulky waste

We collect bulky waste for Kommuneqarfik Sermersooq twice a year, usually in June and October. Dates are announced in Nuuk Ugeavis. Need a pickup in between? Call us for a good price.

Vacuum trucks & liquid waste

If you as a homeowner or business are responsible for emptying your tank, we collect all types of liquid waste and sludge in our modern ADR-approved vehicle. Our vacuum trucks are Berit, Beth and Birthe. Call +299 38 40 90.

Waste oil (ADR)

Transport of waste oil with ADR-approved vehicles. We have the largest and most modern ADR-approved vehicle for collecting and transporting liquid waste in Greenland.

Pressure jetting

Jetting and cleaning of sewers and of boat and ship hulls.

Construction waste

Open containers for construction waste (metal and wood) for contractors and private builds.
