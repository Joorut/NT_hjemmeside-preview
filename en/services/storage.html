Storage

Outdoors, frost-free or chilled. We have room for containers, equipment, building materials and goods, short or long term.

Book storage

Outdoor storage in Qinngorput

15,000 m² of paved storage area with access control and monitoring at Ilisivik. Ideal for containers, machinery and building materials.

Frost-free hall at the harbour

800 m² frost-free hall with 8 m clearance at Nuuk harbour, for goods that cannot freeze.

Indoor and chilled

Indoor and chilled storage at Nuuk harbour, close to the quay, so goods can move straight to and from the vessel.

Facilities

Room for everything

  • 15,000 m² outdoor storage on paved ground with access control in Qinngorput (Ilisivik)
  • Frost-free hall of 800 m² with 8 m clearance at Nuuk harbour
  • Indoor and chilled storage at Nuuk harbour
  • 4,000 m² under roof in our harbour building, the former fish factory
  • Environmentally sound wash facility with oil separation, e.g. for washing pipes

Storage terms

Storage can be cancelled in writing to the end of a month with one week’s notice. Storage is at the customer’s own risk, and the areas are private with access control. See the full terms and conditions.
