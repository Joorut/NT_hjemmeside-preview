Port & trawler unloading

We unload around 130 fishing vessels a year in Nuuk, from small boats to large ocean-going trawlers, and we also have an unloading team in Aasiaat.

Book unloadingUnloading calendar
Port services

Everything around your port call

Extensive experience and a highly skilled service network have made Nuuk the best transshipment port in the Davis Strait. We also supply staff for other port-related work.

  • Unloading all types of vessels, including trawlers
  • Container stripping and stuffing
  • Manual handling, thawing and cleaning of cargo
  • Ship watch during your call at Nuuk harbour
  • Waste and service runs for vessels
  • Crew supply for vessels
Ship watch & crew

People for your vessel while it is in port

Book ship watch and crew together with the unloading and you have one contact for the whole port call.

Ship watch

We provide ship watch during your call at Nuuk harbour. It can be booked together with unloading and service runs.

Crew for vessels

Temporary staff and labour for vessels and other port-related work. In recent years we have also supplied staff to the construction industry. About staffing.

Waste and service runs

We collect waste from the vessel and handle other service runs while you are in port.
