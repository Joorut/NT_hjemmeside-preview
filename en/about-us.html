About us

From one crane truck to one of Greenland’s largest transport companies

Our goal is to be the best in our field in Greenland and a trustworthy partner.

Nuuk Transport A/S

Solidity and long experience

Jens Egede started in 1985 with a single crane truck. Unloading at Nuuk harbour, municipal refuse collection, large storage facilities and the Igaffik restaurant followed. Today we are one of Greenland’s largest transport companies and the country’s largest waste operator.

We combine solidity and long experience with innovative, environmentally sound solutions, from Greenland’s largest mobile crane to modern compactor trucks and ADR-approved vehicles.

Nuuk Transport is part of the Niclasen group, which also runs the workshop company Saffiorfik A/S.

History

Milestones since 1985

  1. 1985

    Jens Egede buys a crane truck and starts as an independent haulier under the name Nuuk Transport, with 1–3 staff.

  2. 1989

    Unloading at Nuuk harbour begins under the name Usit Nuuk ApS. The company grows to around 30 staff.

  3. 1996

    The company becomes a private limited company named Nuuk Transport og Usit Nuuk ApS.

  4. 1998

    Municipal refuse collection is taken over and the company has around 50 staff. Edward Niclasen becomes partner and co-owner, and Nuuk Transport becomes the sole unloader of fishing vessels at Nuuk harbour. The first side loader is bought.

  5. 2001

    Refuse collection switches to a new, environmentally friendly concept, and municipal night-soil collection is taken over.

  6. 2002

    Refuse collection for the INI housing company is added, and we become the country’s largest waste operator. We still are.

  7. 2003

    The former GFI fish factory at the harbour is bought (4,000 m² under roof) and the Igaffik restaurant opens.

  8. 2009

    The company becomes Nuuk Transport A/S with 110 employees, now one of Greenland’s largest transport companies.

  9. 2010

    Work begins on Ilisivik, our outdoor storage in Qinngorput. The same year we start working for the oil exploration industry.

  10. 2012

    Greenland’s largest mobile crane makes larger and taller prefabricated buildings possible in Nuuk, such as Nuuk Center.

  11. 2013

    Ilisivik is completed with monitored storage and an environmentally sound wash facility with oil separation. A new 800 m² frost-free hall with 8 m clearance opens at the harbour.

  12. Today

    Nuuk Transport is part of the Niclasen group, covering transport, cranes, stevedoring in Nuuk and Aasiaat, waste collection and catering.

Responsibility

Responsibility for the environment and society

We work with environmentally sound solutions across the business and support local initiatives in Greenland.

  • The municipality’s refuse costs were halved when we became the operator
  • Greenland’s largest and most modern ADR-approved vehicle for liquid waste
  • Environmentally sound wash facility with oil separation at Ilisivik
  • Crane and forklift certification for our staff

Sponsorship policy

We support initiatives in Greenland that benefit larger groups of people, particularly in environment and health and for disadvantaged citizens. In special cases individuals with special needs, such as illness, can be supported. Support is usually given as goods or services.

We do not support travel, study stays, activities outside Greenland or projects paying organisers.

Send an application clearly describing the purpose and use of the support to nuuk@transport.gl. Adopted by the board on 19 January 2013.

Training

Certified, skilled staff

We buy certification courses from EUC Nordvestsjælland so our staff have the right qualifications and meet Working Environment Authority requirements.

  • Mobile crane certificate: operating mobile cranes from 8 to over 30 tonne-metres, slinging, load charts and stability
  • Forklift certificate: safe operation of forklifts and stackers in warehouses, containers and on ramps
